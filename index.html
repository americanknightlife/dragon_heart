Parent & Educator Guide

Educational Philosophy: This project uses "The Feynman Technique" (teaching a concept to others to master it) and habit-tracking to build emotional intelligence.
  • Consistency Over Intensity: The 90-day egg stage is designed to make positivity a habit. It requires very little time each day, but high consistency.
  • Reflective Learning: In the Baby stage, when the child "teaches" the dragon what they learned in school, they are performing metacognition—thinking about their own learning.
  • Data Ownership: This game is "Serverless." No data is ever sent to the cloud. Your child's thoughts remain on their device.
Play Anywhere

Play Offline & Local

You don't need to be online to raise your dragon! You can download the entire game and keep it on a computer, a tablet, or even a USB drive.

How to Download & Play Locally:

  • Go to the repository: GitHub Project Link
  • Find the green button that says "Code" (near the top right).
  • Click "Download ZIP" from the menu.
  • Find the file in your computer's "Downloads" folder and Extract (unzip) it.
  • Double-click index.html. The game will open in your web browser, but it is running entirely from your own files!

Important: The "Dragon Soul" File

When playing locally, browsers might sometimes clear their "Local Storage" memory. To make sure you never lose your dragon:

  • Click "Save Soul" inside the game frequently. This downloads a .json file.
  • If you move to a new computer or the game looks empty, click "Load Soul" and select your latest file.
  • Think of this file as your dragon's Spirit and Memory. Keep it safe!
